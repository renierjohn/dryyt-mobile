Dryyt Payouts

What a shop owner sees on the app's Payouts screen: four tabs, what each amount means, how it's worked out, and which tables it reads or writes. Amounts are stored in centavos and every computed fee is rounded up to a whole peso. Days, weeks and months are Manila time.

Overview

Payouts is the owner's money screen. Swipe left or right, or tap a tab, to switch.

APP FeeWhat the shop owes Dryyt, and what it has paid DropoffDrivers' fees for confirmed drop offs PickupDrivers' share of delivered laundry pickups OrdersDrivers' share of delivered food orders

The Dropoff, Pickup and Orders tabs load 10 rows at a time, newest first, with Show More. They are grouped by day. The APP Fee tab has a month summary at the top and the service fees grouped by week below it.

Where the money comes from

Every delivery (a drop off or a pickup) is priced from the area's rate in rates and the road distance:

delivery_fee = round_up(base_fee + road_km × rate_per_km) → the driver's share service_fee = round_up(delivery_fee × commission_pct / 100) → Dryyt's cut total_delivery_fee = delivery_fee + service_fee → what the customer pays for delivery

The customer pays the shop: for a pickup, the items (food) or laundry fee plus total_delivery_fee, upfront to the shop's payment QR; for a drop off, the laundry fee plus the delivery fee. The shop then owes:

Pay (send proof)

The + Pay button opens a small form: reference number, or, upload proof of payment, then Send. Either a reference number or a photo is enough.

  1. The photo (if any) is shrunk and uploaded: POST /owner/app-fee/photo. It's stored in R2 (PROOFS) under app-fee/<owner id>/.
  2. POST /owner/app-fee adds a row to app_fee: date_paid = today, ref_number, photo, with amount 0 and type NULL.
  3. It shows as For review until an admin fills it in from the cpanel (see Cpanel side).

Weekly service fee

Dryyt's service_fee from every finished delivery at the owner's shops, grouped by week (Monday to Sunday). Same rule as the cpanel's Stores tab, so the two always match:

  • Pickups (food orders and laundry pickups) with status = 'delivered'
  • Drop offs with status = 'completed' (not ones still waiting for payment)

Dropoff tab

What the shop owes drivers for drop offs. When the owner taps Confirm received on a drop off (POST /owner/transactions/:id/confirm), one batch:

  • adds a payouts row for the driver: amount = transactions_dropoff.delivery_fee, status pending;
  • adds a payouts row for Dryyt (payee_type = 'developer'): amount = transactions_dropoff.service_fee;
  • adds the delivery fee to the driver's running total, driver_data.earnings;
  • marks owner_transactions.status = 'ok' and transactions_dropoff.status = 'owner_confirmed'.

Orders tab

Exactly like the Pickup tab, for food orders (GET /owner/pickup-payouts?type=food): the driver's delivery_fee for each delivered food order, paid or pending, with the same Mark paid.

Cpanel side

  • Stores tab / Owners → Stores popup: set each shop's fee (fee_type, fee_flat, fee_percentage). This is what Amount Due is worked out from.
  • Owners → Subscription popup: proofs the owner sent appear above the payments table (photo or "No photo", the reference number on hover). Click one to see it and fill in amount, type, payment type, date paid and next due (POST /admin/app-fee/:id). It then moves into the table and becomes the owner's latest payment. + Record payment adds one directly (POST /admin/owners/:id/fees).
  • Owners list: the Subscription column shows the last amount paid, overdue past the next due date, and "N to review" while proofs wait.
